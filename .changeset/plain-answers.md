The explainer also answers for an owner who is not a developer: `Explanation.plain` holds the bottom line and four answers in plain words (can the agent overspend, what actually stops it, what could still go wrong, what to do next), all five or none. An explanation can be asked for in English, Spanish or Portuguese (`language` on `explain()`, `POST /v1/explain`, the `alma_explain` tool, and `explain --language`); check ids, verdict names and code stay as they are. As before, the verdict, the findings and each fix are the checks', and wording that reassures beyond the verdict is flagged.
